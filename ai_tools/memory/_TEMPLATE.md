## The rule

One or two sentences, in the imperative. Something a reviewer can hold a diff
against.

## Why

The reasoning that made it the right call — including what we gave up. This is
the part that stops the decision being silently reversed in six months by
someone who only sees the cost of keeping it.

## What it looks like in the code

Where it is enforced, and what violating it would look like. Point at files
rather than pasting code, which goes stale.

## When to revisit

The condition that would make this wrong. If there isn't one, say so.
