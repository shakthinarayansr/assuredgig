## What

The change, in two or three sentences. Concrete enough that approving it is a
decision and not an agreement in principle.

## Why now

What made this come up. If it came out of a correction, link the `reasoning/`
entry — a proposal with evidence behind it is a different conversation from one
without.

## Cost of not doing it

Be specific and honest, including "nothing much, but it will keep costing us a
little". The proposals that get deferred forever are the ones whose cost was
never written down.

## Options

1. **Preferred** — and why.
2. The alternative, and what it trades away.
